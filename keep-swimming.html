How to play

  • ←→ or AD to swim
  • On a phone, hold the arrows or either side of the pool
  • P or Esc to pause, Space to start
  • F for full screen (phones go full screen when you start)

Collect

  • Tokens: +10 each, multiplied by your context streak
  • Checkpoint: survives one crash
  • Heart: an extra life, up to three

Avoid

  • CUDA OOM: the GPU ran out of memory
  • Adversarial patch: noise that fools vision models
  • Hallucination: drifts side to side
  • Exploding gradient: bounces off the walls
